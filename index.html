About

Imagine a late-night drive down a fog-laced backroad, the headlights barely cutting through the mist.

That’s where Kilynn Ross lives: warm, haunting, emotionally honest songs where indie folk meets indie rock. Shimmering guitars and gentle fingerpicking drift around a deep baritone voice that feels like worn leather and campfire smoke. The songs start close and quiet, almost a whisper, then slowly swell into something wide and warm.

It’s music for quiet porch nights, long drives, and those moments when you’re sitting with what it really means to love, lose, and keep going.

For fans of The National, Bon Iver and early Fleet Foxes. On Bridge Recording, Zoe, Oklahoma.
